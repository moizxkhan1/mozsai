How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After a short call we send you a clear plan and price in writing, before you commit to anything.

Who actually works on my project?

One of the founders leads it, with engineers, designers and artists from our team doing the build. You'll always know who's on it.

Who owns the work?

You do. All code, designs, assets and IP belong to you.

How do you use AI?

To speed up the repetitive parts of engineering. People make the decisions and review every piece of work, and we never ship AI-generated art or content without your approval.

Can you pick up a project someone else started?

Yes. We start by reviewing what already exists, so we both know what we're taking on.

Which engines and tools do you work with?

Unreal Engine and Unity for games. For products, modern web technologies and the major AI providers, chosen to fit your project rather than lock you in.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it over before the first call.
